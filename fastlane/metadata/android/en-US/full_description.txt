<p>DissPair is an open-source Bluetooth security toolkit for auditing <b>Bluetooth Classic (RFCOMM)</b> and <b>Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)</b> devices you own or are authorized to test. Instead of trusting what a device advertises, it actively probes how the device really behaves. Two CVEs have been found with it (CVE-2026-31280, CVE-2025-67133).</p>

<p><b>Discovery</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Scan Classic (BR/EDR) and BLE devices, plus already-paired devices</li>
<li>Device class tags, RSSI and filters for unnamed and paired devices</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Bluetooth Classic (RFCOMM)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Sweep RFCOMM channels 1-30 and see which accept unpaired (insecure) or paired connections</li>
<li>Send AT commands and OBEX payloads, with automatic HFP handshake handling</li>
<li>Flood test to stress a channel's buffer handling</li>
</ul>

<p><b>BLE advertisement inspector (GAP)</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Decodes every advertising field: flags, service UUIDs, names, TX power, appearance, service and manufacturer data</li>
<li>Vendor formats: Apple Continuity (iBeacon, AirDrop, Handoff, Nearby Info, Find My), Eddystone, Google Fast Pair</li>
<li>Privacy checks: public vs rotating addresses, identifiers in device names, tracker beacons (Find My, SmartTag, Tile)</li>
<li>Advertising interval estimate, RSSI graph, legacy/extended PDU, PHY and connectable flags</li>
</ul>

<p><b>BLE GATT analysis</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Full service and characteristic map with standard and vendor names</li>
<li>Read and write (with or without response), hex or text</li>
<li>Subscribe to notifications and indications with a live log</li>
<li>Automatic maximum MTU negotiation</li>
</ul>

<p><b>GATT access audit</b></p>
<ul>
<li>One tap tests what every characteristic allows without pairing: read, subscribe and write</li>
<li>Results marked OPEN, AUTH, ENC, AUTHZ, KEY or DENY</li>
<li>Optional write test: echo the value just read, or commit a chosen payload</li>
<li>Exportable text report for vendor disclosure</li>
</ul>

<p>Follows the system light or dark theme. A companion Linux CLI is available in the source repository.</p>

<p><b>Warning:</b> some features (flooding, payload injection, writes) can disrupt or reconfigure the target device. Use only on hardware you own or have explicit permission to test.</p>
